# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Primary users are engaged couples who need a simple wedding RSVP website they can launch quickly, plus guests who will usually open the site from mobile chat apps and social links. A secondary audience is wedding vendors or referrals who may judge SunSpire Studios by the polish and clarity of this sample.

## Product Purpose
This project demonstrates SunSpire Studios' Starter wedding RSVP package: a one-page invitation site that gives guests the essential event details, lets them respond quickly, and shows couples that speed and affordability can still feel polished. Success means the page feels easy to share, easy to understand on mobile, and credible as a sellable sample.

## Brand Personality
Practical, warm, and composed. The tone should feel reassuring rather than extravagant: personal enough for a wedding invitation, but clear enough that guests can act without friction. The emotional goal is confidence with a soft romantic edge.

## Anti-references
- Overbuilt luxury wedding websites with too many sections, flourishes, or storytelling detours.
- Generic SaaS-looking landing pages with card grids, stat blocks, and marketing filler.
- Dark, moody, or ornate invitation styles that make the Starter package feel expensive or hard to use.

## Design Principles
- Put RSVP and event clarity first; beauty should support action.
- Make the first mobile impression feel personal and photo-led.
- Keep the scope intentionally light so the Starter package feels fast and affordable.
- Use typography, spacing, and imagery to create polish instead of decorative clutter.
- Let every section earn its place in the scroll.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target a clean, readable experience with strong text contrast, large touch targets, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support. Mobile readability is the primary accessibility concern because guests are likely to open the invitation from phones.
